# Contributing to HotLoop

Thanks for your interest in HotLoop. A few things to know before you dig in.

## Where things live

- **HotLoop Flow:** [`hotloop-flow`](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/hotloop-flow)
- **HotLoop IoT, Edge, Gateway and Edge Relay:** `hotloop`, in this org and private for now. Until it opens up, bugs and ideas for them go in Discussions.
- **HotLoop Cascade:** `hotloop-cascade`, also private for now. Same deal, Discussions.
- **Website:** [`HotLoop-io.github.io`](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io.github.io)
- **Docs:** [`docs`](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/docs)
- **Community chatter, questions, and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/HotLoop-io/discussions)

## Reporting bugs and requesting features

Open an issue on the relevant repo using the templates provided. Include enough detail, steps to reproduce, environment, and logs, that someone else can act on it without a follow-up round trip.

## Pull requests

- Keep PRs focused on one change.
- Explain the why, not just the what, in the description.
- By submitting a change, you agree it's licensed under the same terms as the repo you're contributing to. See that repo's `LICENSE`.
