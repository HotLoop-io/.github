## What this actually is

Most home automation tools are toys. They work fine until you ask them to do something real, and then they fall over. Most industrial automation platforms are the opposite problem. They actually work, but they cost a fortune, take a team to configure, and no regular person is ever getting near one.

HotLoop is what happens when you refuse to accept that split. It's a real automation platform, built around an actual loop: sense what's happening, decide what to do about it, act on it, and do it again, continuously, without babysitting it. That loop is the whole point. It's the difference between "this triggered once and I hope it works" and something you can actually trust to run your house or your equipment while you're not looking at it.

We built it so the same platform runs a single sensor in somebody's garage and a full floor of industrial gear. No dumbed down home version, no bloated enterprise version. One tool, built properly, for whoever needs it.

## Who gets it for free and who doesn't

If you're an individual, this is free. Full stop. Run it in your house, run it in your garage, run it for a class, run it for your nonprofit, tinker with it because you feel like it. No trial, no countdown, no "upgrade now" popup. That's the deal and we're not going back on it.

If you're a business, you don't get it from here. HotLoop's business release is handled exclusively through our partner [Embernet](https://embernet.ai). It doesn't matter if it's internal only, customer facing, making you money, or costing you money. If a business is running it, that business goes through Embernet, period. This isn't a cheap trick where we cripple the free version until you pay us. It's a real partnership, and it means a business actually gets support, accountability, and someone who picks up the phone when something breaks, instead of a GitHub issue that sits for six months.

If you genuinely don't know which side of that line you're on, ask before you build a house on the wrong foundation. Email support@hotloop.io, or just go talk to Embernet if it smells like a business case.

The [full license](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io/blob/main/LICENSE.md) is the actual legal document and it wins every argument. Everything above is us explaining it like human beings instead of lawyers.
